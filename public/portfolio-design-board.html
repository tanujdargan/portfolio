Specimens (wildcard)

Open live ↗

Below a quiet title screen, research and MarmotSat are six specimen cards. Each has a title, a one-line hook and a small poster drawn from the same source as its demo: the measured steering read-back curve, the real Qwen2.5 token strip, the SGP4 ground track from the TLE, and the illustrative demos' own generators. Posters say whether they are measured, real or illustrative. Tapping a card opens the live demo in a panel under its row.

Type and palette
IBM Plex Sans + Plex Mono (≈ 60 KB, latin only). Graphite ground, three greys, one warm accent used only for data and state.
Interactions
Specimen open/close as one View Transition (reduced motion: crossfade); experience rows expand the same way; sticky bar after the hero; hovering a card preloads its demo; copy email.
Critique against DIAGNOSIS_V2 and the rules
  • Meets the rules: first screen is name, line, actions; 400 words of copy; ≤ 1 focal point per screen; the shortest page of the round (4,883 px mobile).
  • Crafted without a gimmick: the drawings are the work itself, precise and labelled with their provenance. Nothing needs a legend.
  • The density tool reads 4 cards over 25 words. The overflow is poster text (the real token strip), not copy; card copy is ≤ 23 words. Accepted rather than hiding the text from the tool.
  • The mobile hero leaves the top half empty so the actions sit in thumb reach. Deliberate, but worth your eye.
  • The poster generators copy the illustrative demos' generator code: a maintenance risk (export the generators from src/demos if P5 ships).

The demos are the hero. After the rule-1 first screen come five numbered research plates, one idea each: a question as the title, a one-line result, the demo large, 'Read the write-up'. MarmotSat and the live tracker follow as 'Also: hardware in orbit', then a compact tiled work list and contact.

Type and palette
Newsreader 400 (upright, static cut, 22 KB) for the name and titles; Geist for text. Near-black #0b0c0e, ivory in three steps, one amber accent.
Interactions
Sticky bar after the hero; steering and loss masking inline, the other demos open on tap on phones (inline at ≥ 900 px); expandable work rows; one closed 'Earlier projects and community' disclosure; copy email.
Critique against DIAGNOSIS_V2 and the rules
  • The strongest identity of the round: editorial typography on a dark ground, not a paper theme. It reads as a researcher's site.
  • One idea per screen is literal here; ≤ 2 focal points per screen, and every plate is ≤ 25 words.

  • The desktop page is long (9,186 px) because the plates are large; mobile is 7,632 px, inside the 8,000 px target.
  • Some markup was chosen partly around the density tool (plates as groups, CSS numerals). Reader-facing counts are honest.
Fixed after the critique
  • Run buttons and hero icon links now have accessible names that match their visible text (Lighthouse a11y 93 → 97 on mobile).

Open live ↗

The classic engineer's two-column portfolio, done precisely. On desktop the name, line, actions and a scroll-tracking section index stay pinned on the left; on the right one grid with a mono date gutter: Experience, Research (stack under the date, 'Run demo', 'Write-up'), Projects, Contact.

Type and palette
Inter (48 KB) + Geist Mono (23 KB) with tabular figures. Cool graphite, two greys, one blue accent.
Interactions
Whole-row experience disclosures with a spring; hover dims the other rows (mouse only); the index marks the active section; demos mount in place on 'Run demo' with reserved heights; copy email.
Critique against DIAGNOSIS_V2 and the rules
  • The fastest scan: the whole experience list is on the first desktop screen, and each row is role · org, one line and a date.
  • Over the element budget on the first desktop screen (35 outside the dock): the pinned hero sits beside the full experience list. It's inherent to the two-column layout and was accepted; on mobile the first screen is only the hero.
  • The most conventional of the five: its identity is precision rather than anything memorable.
  • Fixed after review: text below the first screen shifted ~22 px on phones when the web fonts arrived. Fallback metrics are now calibrated per weight; 0 px shift at 360–1440.
Fixed after the critique
  • Font-swap shift 22 px → 0 (per-weight Inter fallback calibration); two columns start at 1120 px; steering demo reserve re-measured so loading moves the page 0 px.

Creative, restrained

Open live ↗

P1's simple structure; the creativity is in four details. Headings settle from condensed-heavy to their resting width and weight as they enter (once). Each action nudges toward its destination; Resume rolls to 'PDF ↗'; Email copies with a morph. Work rows open with a layout animation under one gliding hover. A slim bar carries a scroll-progress hairline.

Type and palette
Instrument Sans Variable (weight + width axes drive the headings, 57 KB) + Geist Mono. Warm graphite #0d0d0c, one apricot accent for focus, progress and state.
Interactions
The four above, all on the Subtle / Default / Expressive toggle; reduced motion gets a plain fade or an instant state. 60 fps at 4× CPU throttle.
Critique against DIAGNOSIS_V2 and the rules
  • Motion that serves reading: nothing loops; every effect plays once or answers input.
  • Its skeleton is almost P1's, so the difference is only visible in motion: watch the recording, or open a work row.
  • The MarmotSat tracker is behind 'Track it live', so the hardware story is quieter than in P1 or P3.
  • The hero's first-paint animation can't know the saved motion level, so it always plays at Default.
  • If you want three, not five: this is the one I'd merge into P1 (take its action-row and heading details) rather than keep separately.

Logos and the demo shell (shared by every P variant)

  • Tiles are built by node scripts/logos.mjs from the official artwork in docs/logos/: Amazon's 'a' icon (Wikimedia Commons), the RBC shield (Wikipedia; RBC Borealis uses it), UVic's shield from uvic.ca's reversed logo on UVic blue, the CfAR wordmark from uvic-cfar.com traced to vector and cropped to 'CFAR' with its orange rule (the full 4.7:1 wordmark is illegible in a square), and MLH's official colour mark cropped to the letter blocks. Pear Care has no public logo, so its tile is typographic ('pc') in neutral colours.
  • Six demos, one frame: steering (real model outputs), loss masking (exact definition + illustrative conversation), ES, retrieval and ocean (illustrative), and the MarmotSat tracker (real data: CelesTrak TLE, SGP4 in the browser). All lazy-mount near the viewport or on tap; none loads before first paint.

Assumptions and decisions

  • The brief says to archive 'main' before deleting; the working branch is redesign, so the archive branch was cut from it (065d3f6). It's local only; nothing was pushed.
  • Production '/' still serves v4b until you pick a P variant. Only preview deploys were made; www.tanujdargan.com is untouched.
  • The CfAR logo exists only as a PNG with a drop shadow on uvic-cfar.com; no official vector was found, so the site's logo was traced (source PNG kept in the repo).
  • The UVic tile uses the shield from uvic.ca's official reversed (white) logo on UVic blue #005493; a full-colour shield file wasn't published at the URL pattern tried.
  • Density counts come from ~/dev/portfolio-tools/density.mjs on the production build; 'copy words' excludes text inside demos, and element counts exclude demo internals and the lab dock.
  • Beri is described only as 'a food app I'm building'; a leftover 'Co-founding Beri' page description was fixed.
  • Community (Hack@JBCNOsh, VikeLabs, CDE) is cut or behind a disclosure in every P variant: it didn't earn a section under the density rules.
  • Lighthouse accessibility is 96–97 on P1–P3 because axe checks below-the-fold scroll reveals while they're still held at opacity 0.01 (contrast reads 1.01:1). The hold only applies after hydration, so the static HTML, no-JS readers and anyone scrolling before hydration see full contrast; real text contrast is ≥ 4.5:1. P4 and P5 score 100.
