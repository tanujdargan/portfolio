Portfolio design board

Studio round. Five new variants (S1–S5) built after recording nine studio sites live (STUDIO_TASTE.md) and collecting 59 motion patterns (research/PATTERNS.md). Principle: restraint in information, richness in motion; the work is the hero and the name is small. Every variant keeps v6/P2's DNA (dark, mono for facts, a visible index, real data labelled), the same six live demos in one shared frame, full-colour logo tiles, and compact links on the first screen. Generated 2026-09-28.

Open live ↗

P2's two-column engineer's site elevated to studio polish. The right column opens on the work: six titles at 72px, each a live index row. The name is a 14px signature in the pinned column.

Type and palette

Critique against STUDIO_TASTE.md

  • Is the work the hero? Yes: the six titles are the biggest thing on the page and the name is 14px.
Fixed after the critique
  • Phone ALSO/EARLIER label overlapped its row title (gutter too narrow); gave the row its own label column.

Known issues

Signature interaction

Click and the row opens in place into an exhibit: a spec sheet whose rules draw and whose result decodes, beside the live demo. On phones the index pins and scroll scrubs through it (basement's accordion); tap opens a full-screen sheet.

Section heads rise out of word masks; experience rows arrive with their rules drawing; a mono readout in the pinned column re-scrambles to name what you're hovering; Geist weight settles on load.

Latent

Open live ↗

v6 elevated: the first screen is the experiment. Real Qwen2.5-1.5B activations fill a WebGL field, and the model's real answer is set large beside it. No name headline (13px mono, top left). Five stops of work, then experience and contact.

Type and palette
Geist Mono + Newsreader (64 KB, subset). Near-black #09090a; the accent re-themes to the work in view (orange/cyan for steering's sign).
Critique against STUDIO_TASTE.md
  • Would it sit next to the studios? Yes. It has the one thing none of them has: the hero is a real experiment that responds. The first screen is the strongest in the round.
  • Too plain? No. Too crowded? The hero carries a label, prompt, sentence, rail, chips, read-back and two buttons; it's dense but every item is part of one instrument. Below the hero, desktop sections are a left column over the field, which reads as air rather than emptiness because the field moves with scroll.
  • Is the work the hero? Literally: the first thing you do is run the research.
  • v6's failures fixed: no giant name, no duplicate steering controls, no 58svh gaps, experience rows ≤ 25 words.
Fixed after the critique
  • Sections scrolled visibly under the transparent top bar (a 'Run the demo' ghost next to the name); the bar now has a real scrim and passes pointer events through its empty area.
  • Lighthouse: the hero sentence used text-wrap: pretty, which re-balanced earlier lines as the decoder typed, moving unchanged words (CLS 0.049 desktop). Greedy wrap and a fixed-height box: 0.005.
  • Accessibility: section stop labels are now the h2s (work titles were h3 under an h1), the prompt button's name contains its visible text, the icon-only Resume link on phones has a name, and the hidden duplicate demo buttons are out of the a11y tree.
Known issues
During a very fast drag the sentence can be empty for a frame between rewrites. The rail thumb is custom-styled in Chromium and Firefox only (Safari gets the native thumb). The density tool flags each work card at 40–51 words because the per-letter roll splits button labels into single characters; the real copy is ~20 words plus button labels. Lighthouse a11y 96–97 is the reveal-hold artifact (below-fold labels at opacity 0.01 when axe reads them).

Signature interaction

Drag the model's mood. Drag the α rail and the field bends and splits in real time while the sentence rewrites itself like a greedy decoder: shared words stay, the rest backspaces, and the new real output streams in, tinted by α's sign. From 'As an AI language model, I do not have personal experiences…' at −1.2 to 'Oh, the sun sparkled with diamonds!' at +1.2. The read-back projection counts, pinned cluster labels follow the bend, and the cursor glow shifts cyan→orange. On phones the rail is a 44 px thumb under the sentence that never traps vertical scroll, with haptic detents where supported.

Transitions and type motion
Into a write-up: the title ghosts, the page dissolves and the points rush past in the work's accent (warp), then the title lands as the article title; back flies you in from the far field. 'Run the demo' grows a glass panel from the button. Section titles decode from glyph noise, hooks rise word by word, posters draw their strokes, only changed characters scramble in the α label. Numbered dock (01–06) with poster preview cards.
Studio references it drew on
v6 (owner's favourite), numbered dock with preview cards (P16, austensor), decode (P2/P31), cursor label (P33), number ticker (P52), code/readout sync (P53), haptics (P54); Unseen (routes as camera moves), Neon (per-work re-theme), basement/Kode (one accent as an event), Koto two-tone titles.

Board

Open live ↗

Experimental navigation. Home is a zoomable research board on a dot grid. The server sends a plain list (complete without JS); after load the list assembles into the board, each card flying from its list slot to its board slot.

Type and palette
IBM Plex Sans Variable (wdth + wght) + Plex Mono (~80 KB). Blue-grey near-black, dots at 7% white; a work's colour appears only when hovered, open, or on the minimap.
Critique against STUDIO_TASTE.md
  • Would it sit next to the studios? On desktop, yes: it looks like a lab's instrument, and the fly-in is a genuine 'show a friend' moment.
  • Too plain? No. Too crowded? The HUD (links, coords, zoom, minimap, keys, hint) is a lot of chrome at once; it's small and mono, but it's the busiest first screen of the five.
  • Is the work the hero? Yes: six posters drawn from real data are the board.
  • Risk: canvas navigation is unfamiliar to recruiters. The List view is one tap away and is what reduced motion and no-JS get.
Fixed after the critique
  • Phone hint sat on the first card's hook at the bottom of the screen; moved under the HUD (a lab-only override had also stretched it into a tall blurred block).
Known issues
Checked in Chromium (desktop and touch emulation) only; real iOS Safari pinch and backdrop blur need a device pass. Dotted links can cross a card on hover. The demo 'ready' moment is a fixed 480 ms after its chunk loads.

Signature interaction

Fly in and it comes alive. Click a card, press 1–6 or tap a chip: the camera flies (log-space zoom, ~700 ms) to frame it, a panel grows from the card and the live demo fades in over the poster while the rest of the board dims and panning locks. Esc flies back out. Semantic zoom: far out, cards are poster + big number; closer in, hooks and metrics fade in and the data draws itself; titles change width with zoom. Hover draws dotted links to related work and to the matching experience tile.

Transitions and type motion
List ⇄ Board is one FLIP in both directions. 'Read the write-up' morphs the panel into the article's poster plate and the title into the article title; back lands framed on that card, then pulls out. Context label scrambles; metrics decode; minimap and HUD fade in. Keys: 1–6, 0/Esc, arrows, ±, T for a tour; Tab flies to the focused card.
Studio references it drew on
Drag/zoom canvas of the work (P47), view-mode toggle (P48), keyboard chips + auto tour (P45), tick minimap (P30), one-lit-thing (Koto), spec plate (basement), a single authored object (Shader); Mobile equivalents 2, 5, 8.

Signal

Open live ↗

Experimental shader. The home page is a receiver: a full-bleed WebGL screen shows the in-focus work's poster as a 1-bit Bayer-dithered signal, with the work's accent on its accent strokes. Each work is a channel.

Type and palette
Mona Sans (wdth/wght) + Martian Mono HUD, subset (62 + 17 KB). Ground #050506, dither grey; colour appears only per channel.
Critique against STUDIO_TASTE.md
  • Would it sit next to the studios? Yes, it looks the most like one of them. The dithered steering curve at display size is the round's best single image.
  • Too plain? No. Too loud? The mid-switch frame (RGB split + snow) is the one place I'd tone down: it's close to a glitch-art cliché, and it only lasts 700 ms, but it's the frame people will screenshot. Easy to halve.
  • Is the work the hero? Yes, every channel is a work's real data drawn huge.
  • On phones the pinned screen leaves a quiet band between the channel and the hook once the index has scrolled.
Fixed after the critique
  • Phone: the first section printed on top of the pinned screen's hook as it scrolled in; sections now carry a full-bleed ground and slide over the pin.
  • Lighthouse: write-up CLS 0.143 on phones. Tag chips fit on one line in the Menlo fallback but wrapped once Martian Mono loaded; the fallback's size-adjust is now measured against the chips (113% → 116.5%). CLS 0.
Known issues
The first WebGL frame is a little sparser than the CSS-dithered first paint, so startup density shifts slightly. The wipe label sits under the lab dock on phones (lab only).

Signature interaction

Channel switch + lens. Hover or focus a row in the Mona Sans index: it widens to wdth 125 / wght 700 while the others condense, and the screen switches channel: one uniform drives macroblock noise, row tearing, an RGB-dithered split and a burst of snow that peak mid-way (700 ms), while the channel label rescrambles. The pointer is a lens that shows the un-dithered, full-colour signal inside a glass circle. Tune in: the dither resolves pixel by pixel in Bayer order to the full signal while a sheet slides up with the live demo. On phones scroll is the tuning dial.

Transitions and type motion
Write-ups: a full-screen wipe in the channel's accent whose leading edge dissolves in Bayer order; back resumes that channel. Section titles open from wdth 75 to 100 per letter; kickers decode; rows draw in top-down in steps.
Studio references it drew on
Text index over a swapping background (P13, Nite Riot), numbered hotspots with shader crossfade (P15, Thibault Guignand), one-uniform block/displacement/chromatic crossfade (P38), Bayer dither (P39), pointer reveal lens (P42), variable-font index (P7); Kode's accent wipe, Neon's per-item accent and progress bar, basement's dithered hero, Shader's single fiction.

Spec

Open live ↗

The calmest variant, alive through focus and telemetry. Koto's sticky two-tone caption column ('Selected work / Steering') beside six live demos, only the centred one lit; a basement spec sheet; an instrument bar with a live UTC clock and MarmotSat's real sub-satellite point.

Type and palette
Instrument Sans (condensed display capped at 48 px) + JetBrains Mono (97 KB). #060606, greys #e8e8e8 / #989898 / #7a7a7a; each work's accent only on the lit exhibit's hairline and badge; MACHINE is amber #ffb000.
Critique against STUDIO_TASTE.md
  • Would it sit next to the studios? Next to Koto, yes: the same restraint and the same one-lit-thing focus. MACHINE mode is the most original idea in the round and exactly on-brand for an ML engineer.
  • Too plain? At rest, on the first screen, it's the quietest (a caption and a demo panel). The telemetry ticking and the caption morph are what keep it alive; the MACHINE moment is the payoff.
  • Too crowded? ~29 words outside the demo on the first desktop screen, a little over target (the four spec rows cost most).
  • Is the work the hero? Yes: the live demos are the exhibits, full size, in order.
Fixed after the critique
  • Brief's #595959 small text failed 4.5:1; it's kept for rules and ticks only, text uses #7a7a7a.
  • Lighthouse: mobile LCP 3.3 s. The first exhibit's live demo mounted on load and its output paragraph became the LCP element after hydration; the demo now mounts on the first real input (pointer, wheel, key, touch or scroll) while its poster holds the slot at the same height. LCP 1.7 s, CLS 0.001.
  • Lighthouse: write-up CLS 0.127. The condensed display type (font-stretch 80%) fell back to full-width Arial and wrapped onto extra lines until the web font arrived; a condensed-matched fallback (Arial at 77%) keeps the line breaks. CLS 0.001.
  • Accessibility: the HUMAN/MACHINE switch's name now contains its visible text, the chapter-rail and picker numbers moved to #7a7a7a (were 2.9:1), and the X link meets the 24 px target.
Known issues
The orbit demo defaults to 60× simulated time, so it doesn't match the nav's live readout (1×). Pages are long on phones because each demo is inline (they mount as they approach). Lighthouse a11y 97: the dimmed (unlit) exhibits read below 4.5:1 by design (Koto's 20% dim); they light on scroll, focus or click.

Signature interaction

HUMAN / MACHINE. Press the pill (or M): every visible text node turns to glyph noise in a wave from the pill and goes amber, demos drop to labelled outlines, a view transition folds the page into one mono column, and a plain-text, key/value mirror of the site prints itself top to bottom for agents and crawlers, headed by an ASCII world map with MarmotSat's live position, its last and next half-orbit, and Victoria. HUMAN reverses it. The state persists for the session.

Transitions and type motion
The lit exhibit morphs (View Transitions, 700 ms) into the write-up header, whose poster draws in; the article gets a sticky chapter rail with an active dot. The caption's work name scrambles and condenses as each exhibit crosses the centre; spec values scramble; hooks rise word by word; clock and counter digits roll.
Studio references it drew on
Koto focused index + sticky caption + two-tone headings + card→hero morph + chapter rail (K1, K2, K6, K8), live clock (K9), definition-list experience (K10), basement spec sheet and HUMAN/MACHINE mirror, number rollers (P52).

Research behind this round

  • Nine studios recorded in a real browser (desktop 1440×900 and iPhone 15, scroll video, hover, click into a case study, computed styles and bundle scan): notes in research/studios/NOTES-A.md and NOTES-B.md; the synthesis is STUDIO_TASTE.md.
  • Wider technique search (not 'portfolio'): Awwwards SOTD and collections, Codrops mechanics, Mobbin web sections, and live sites such as darkroom.engineering, austensor.com, thibaultguignand.com, gilhuybrecht.com, igloo.inc, lusion.co, leoparpeix.com: research/PATTERNS.md (59 patterns).
  • The expensive recipe in one line: few words; live work instead of thumbnails; one lit thing at a time; tight or capped display type; one ease family; one accent used as an event; transitions that keep continuity; an authored object; a nav that does double duty; a small instrument layer.
  • Captures (45 MB of third-party screenshots and video) stay local in research/ and are gitignored.

Logos and the demo shell (shared by every variant)

  • Tiles are built by node scripts/logos.mjs from the official artwork in docs/logos/: Amazon's 'a' icon (Wikimedia Commons), the RBC shield (Wikipedia; RBC Borealis uses it), UVic's shield from uvic.ca's reversed logo on UVic blue, the CfAR wordmark from uvic-cfar.com traced to vector and cropped to 'CFAR' with its orange rule (the full 4.7:1 wordmark is illegible in a square), and MLH's official colour mark cropped to the letter blocks. Pear Care has no public logo, so its tile is typographic ('pc') in neutral colours.
  • Six demos, one frame: steering (real model outputs), loss masking (exact definition + illustrative conversation), ES, retrieval and ocean (illustrative), and the MarmotSat tracker (real data: CelesTrak TLE, SGP4 in the browser). All lazy-mount near the viewport or on tap; none loads before first paint.

Assumptions and decisions

  • Ambiguous studio names. 'Cloud Studio' → cloudstudio.es (the Awwwards design-agency entry; cloud.studio is an IoT SaaS). 'Neon' → NEON (neonrated.com), the only award-level 'Neon' (a film studio; neon.studio is parked). 'Cofounder' → cofounder.co (an AI product site by Altalogy, listed on Godly); no design studio of that name has a live site. Light-themed sites were used for mechanics only. Swap any of these if you meant a different studio.
  • 'Delete from main' is read as 'from the site and the lab'. The main branch holds the old site and has no variants; all work is on redesign. Nothing was pushed or deployed; production is untouched.
  • Production '/' still serves v4b until you pick. Nothing about the live site changed.
  • Stack kept (React + Vite SSR prerender, framer-motion, three). Added Lenis only; no GSAP (CSS, WAAPI, framer-motion and small rAF loops covered every effect). S2 and S4 use raw WebGL2, and three.js stays confined to the orbit demo.
  • Name is ≤ 16 px in every S variant (nav or pinned signature); no variant has a name headline.
  • Facts come only from site.ts (the Aug 2026 resume and existing content). The six work hooks in content/work.ts are shortened from them. S2's hero sentence and S1/S3/S4/S5 posters come from the same real data as the demos (steering.json, lossmask.json, the MarmotSat TLE); retrieval, ES and ocean remain labelled illustrative.
  • Beri is 'a food app I'm building' everywhere; never founder or co-founder.
  • MarmotSat telemetry (S5) is computed live in the browser with SGP4 from the published TLE (CelesTrak, falling back to the build-time copy).
  • Density is measured on the production build with density.mjs, which now ignores screen-reader duplicates (.sr-only copies made by Split/Scramble). Word counts on S variants exclude text inside demos; the first window includes the lab dock.
  • Recordings are scripted Playwright sessions (headless Chromium with GPU) and are choppier than a real browser; WebKit screenshots render backdrop-filter panels as transparent, which is a capture artifact, not a bug.
  • Lighthouse accessibility below 100 on some S variants comes from axe reading below-the-fold reveals while they're held at opacity 0.01; the static HTML and real contrast are ≥ 4.5:1.
